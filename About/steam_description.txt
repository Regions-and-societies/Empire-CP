[h1]Regions and Societies: Empire Patch[/h1]
Compatibility patch: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3701480464]Empire Refactored[/url] colonies are classified, populated and owned under [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3784666060]Regions and Societies[/url] territory rules, and Empire's settlement placement, reward economy and tithe respect regional ownership.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Typed binding:[/b] Empire colonies are recognised by real type and member - population from actual workers, tier from settlement level. A rename in Empire is a compile error, never a silent zero in-game.
[*] [b]Territory-governed placement:[/b] founding an Empire settlement inside another faction's region is refused, with the reason shown.
[*] [b]Your empire on the map:[/b] the player's empire resolves to Empire's own faction, labelled with Empire's configured name.
[*] [b]Reward and tithe economy:[/b] rewards honour the reward def's filters, tech level and quality ranges, and tithe value reflects real stack value.
[/list]

No settings of its own - all governance toggles live in Regions and Societies. Installed means enabled.
